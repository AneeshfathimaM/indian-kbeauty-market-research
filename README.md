# Indian K-Beauty Market Research

## Overview

This project presents a market research analysis of the growing K-beauty industry in India.

The research examines the development of K-beauty in the Indian market, consumer behavior, digital marketing, influencer marketing, user-generated content (UGC), brand positioning, pricing, opportunities, and challenges.

The study combines published market data, industry research, consumer insights, and brand-level observations to understand how Korean beauty brands are building a presence among Indian consumers.

## Key Areas Covered

- Indian K-beauty market size and growth
- Consumer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Skincare and product preferences
- Social media and digital product discovery
- Influencer marketing and UGC
- Educational beauty content
- K-beauty brand positioning in India
- Product pricing comparison between India and South Korea
- Indian celebrity and cultural marketing campaigns
- Competition within the K-beauty category
- Opportunities and challenges in the Indian market

## Key Findings

- The Indian K-beauty market was valued at ₹3,139.31 crore in 2025.
- The market is projected to reach ₹32,424.08 crore by 2035.
- The market is projected to grow at a 26.30% CAGR during 2026–2035.
- Skincare represents the stronger segment of the K-beauty category in India.
- Gen Z and Millennials form an important consumer group.
- Instagram, YouTube, influencers, and UGC play an important role in product discovery and promotion.
- Korean beauty brands are adapting their marketing through Indian celebrities, cultural occasions, and locally relevant product positioning.

## Research Method

The research was developed using secondary data from:

- Market research reports
- Industry publications
- Consumer research
- Brand websites
- Beauty platforms
- News and business publications
- Published articles and reports on K-beauty and digital marketing

The findings were compared and interpreted to identify major trends in the Indian K-beauty market.
